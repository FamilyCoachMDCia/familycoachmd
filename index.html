Psychiatric care

Physician-led care for children and teens.

Psychiatric medication management. Dr. Che is a medical doctor: she evaluates, prescribes and follows up on medication for children and teens.

When worry, mood, attention or behavior start to take over family life, your child deserves a careful look from a specialist. As a medical doctor, Dr. Che can prescribe and manage medication when it's appropriate, which is what sets her care apart from therapy and coaching. She trained in child and adolescent psychiatry at Creighton and has cared for young people in outpatient clinics, partial hospitalization and residential treatment.

  1. 01

    A free 15-minute call

    Tell us what's going on. We'll be honest about whether we're the right fit, and point you elsewhere if we're not.

  2. 02

    A thorough evaluation

    Time with your child and with you, to understand the whole picture: development, school, family, history and strengths.

  3. 03

    A plan you understand

    Clear next steps for home, school and care, including medication when it's appropriate, and follow-up to see them through.

Practical details: availability for new patients, exact ages, in-person or telehealth visits, and fees or insurance are confirmed with you on the free call, before anything is scheduled.

Group parenting course

Kind and firm, at the same time.

Children don't come with a manual (that would be nice). Positive Discipline, developed by Dr. Jane Nelsen from Adlerian psychology, gives parents and caregivers a respectful, practical way to show up: responsive, respectful and resourceful.

Kind without firm: kids feel loved, but a little lost.

Drag the leaf to find the balance

  1. 01Helps children feel a sense of connection and belonging.
  2. 02Is kind and firm at the same time, building flexibility and resilience.
  3. 03Works for the long term, considering what a child thinks, feels, learns and decides about themselves and their world.
  4. 04Teaches social and life skills: respect, concern for others, problem-solving, communication and cooperation.
  5. 05Invites children to discover how capable they are.

  • Format

    2-hour classes over 3 to 6 weeks, in person or online, with hands-on parent–child role-play.

  • Who

    Parents and caregivers from your school, church or community group, or simply a group of friends.

  • Customize

    Add trauma-informed care and the topics your families need most.

  • Certified

    Medical doctor board certified in child and adolescent psychiatry and certified Positive Discipline parent educator.

Consulting for schools

We help your team C.A.R.E.

We partner with schools to give staff practical tools for lasting improvement, so they can better support students and families.

CARE

  1. Collaborate

    We listen to school leaders, teachers, support staff and families, then work with your team to make changes that last.

  2. Align

    We align on shared goals and everyday expectations across directors, teachers and families, so everyone is working toward the same thing.

  3. Re-imagine

    Together we rethink systems and everyday practices: arranging rooms and materials to invite cooperation and exploration, and knowing when to refer a child for extra support or evaluation.

  4. Educate

    Professional development for staff and directors: adult–child interactions, social-emotional learning, prosocial practices and child development, plus parent courses for your families.

Questions families ask

Frequently asked questions.

Don't see yours? Ask it on your free 15-minute call.

Is coaching the same as therapy or psychiatric care?

No. Coaching is practical, non-clinical support for parenting and family life. Psychiatric care is medical care from a physician, including medication management when it's appropriate. On your free call we'll help you figure out which one fits.

Do you prescribe medication?

Yes. Dr. Che is a medical doctor and prescribes medication for children and teens through psychiatric medication management, which is separate from coaching. Coaching and courses don't include medication. If your child may need medication, or already takes it, we'll talk through the next step on your free call.

Where do you see families?

In the Omaha area. Medication management visits can be in person or by telehealth. Coaching sessions are by video; in-person sessions need to be pre-arranged. Dr. Che is licensed in Nebraska and Iowa.

Can my school offer support to its families?

Yes. Schools can ask about private family coaching and group parenting courses for their families.

Do I need to belong to a school to request a parenting course?

No. We also work with small groups, including playgroups, church groups, book clubs and friends.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the service and the size of your group. We'll share them on your free 15-minute call, with no pressure to decide.

Do you work with families of every background?

Yes. Our approach honors families of every ethnic and sociocultural background.

Is this for emergencies?

No. If your child is in danger, call 911. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988, any time.
