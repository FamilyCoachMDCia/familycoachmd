Psychiatric care

Physician-led care for children and teens.

When worry, mood, attention or behavior start to take over family life, your child deserves a careful look from a specialist. Dr. Che trained in child and adolescent psychiatry at Creighton and has cared for young people in outpatient clinics, partial hospitalization and residential treatment.

  1. 01

    A free 15-minute call

    Tell us what's going on. We'll be honest about whether we're the right fit, and point you elsewhere if we're not.

  2. 02

    A thorough evaluation

    Time with your child and with you, to understand the whole picture: development, school, family, history and strengths.

  3. 03

    A plan you understand

    Clear next steps for home, school and care, and follow-up to see them through.

Practical details: availability for new patients, exact ages, in-person or video visits, and fees or insurance are confirmed with you on the free call, before anything is scheduled.

Private family coaching

Confident parents. Capable children. A compassionate world.

Is this you?

  • Time investment

    Two 1-hour sessions a week for 6 weeks. Family sessions mixed with one-on-one time. By phone or Zoom.

  • Personalized

    We start by understanding your family's dynamics, then build a plan around your unique needs and goals.

  • Whole family

    Invite grandparents, co-parents or anyone outside the home. When helpful, a developmental assessment for children 2½ to 9 (see below).

  • Included

    A comprehensive, trauma-informed assessment, an evidence-based personal plan, a personalized nutrition, mindfulness and exercise plan, unlimited email support and practical tools for your family's obstacles.

Developmental assessment, ages 2½ to 9How the Gesell developmental observation works

When it helps the plan, Dr. Che uses the Gesell Developmental Observation–Revised (GDO-R) to see where a child is in their growth, so goals fit the child rather than an average. It combines time observing your child with input from the adults who know them best: parents and, when it's useful, teachers or caregivers.

  • Thinking and problem-solving
  • Language and communication
  • Physical and neurological development, including movement
  • Social, emotional and adaptive skills
  • Early letters and numbers
  • Visual and spatial perception

It is a developmental observation, not a diagnosis. You'll talk through the results together and decide next steps.

Group parenting course

Kind and firm, at the same time.

Children don't come with a manual (that would be nice). Positive Discipline, developed by Dr. Jane Nelsen from Adlerian psychology, gives parents and caregivers a respectful, practical way to show up: responsive, respectful and resourceful.

Kind without firm: kids feel loved, but a little lost.

Drag the leaf to find the balance

  1. 01Helps children feel a sense of connection and belonging.
  2. 02Is kind and firm at the same time, building flexibility and resilience.
  3. 03Works for the long term, considering what a child thinks, feels, learns and decides about themselves and their world.
  4. 04Teaches social and life skills: respect, concern for others, problem-solving, communication and cooperation.
  5. 05Invites children to discover how capable they are.

  • Format

    2-hour classes over 3 to 6 weeks, in person or online, with hands-on parent–child role-play.

  • Who

    Parents and caregivers from your school, center, church or group, or simply a group of friends.

  • Customize

    Add trauma-informed care and the topics your families need most.

  • Certified

    Dr. Che is a Certified Positive Discipline Parent Educator (2018) and Early Childhood Educator (2021).

Consulting for schools & childcare centers

We help your team C.A.R.E.

We partner with schools and childcare centers to equip staff with the tools to drive meaningful, lasting improvement, so you can better serve the children and families at your learning center.

CARE

  1. Collaborate

    We listen to everyone who shapes your center, from directors and teachers to support staff and families, and work with your team toward positive, sustainable change.

  2. Assess

    We observe children's development and growth with research-based tools, such as the Gesell developmental observation, so support starts from what each child needs.

  3. Re-imagine

    Together we rethink systems and everyday practices: arranging rooms and materials to invite cooperation and exploration, and knowing when to refer a child for extra support or evaluation.

  4. Educate

    Professional development for staff and directors: adult–child interactions, social-emotional learning, prosocial practices and child development, plus parent courses for your families.

Why FamilyCoachMD exists

It all began with a drive to see thriving families, joyful people and empowered kids. FamilyCoachMD was born out of a desire to prevent pathology before it overwhelms an individual, a family, a team, an organization, a system, a community, a nation and the world.

I believe everyone can reach their God-given potential when placed in a thriving environment, be it at home, at school or at work. I've seen it in the clinic, in classrooms and in the libraries we built in Cameroon: when the environment changes, people bloom.

So we partner with you on this journey called life: through coaching for families and organizations, consulting for schools, childcare centers and other organizations, courses for parents, books for children, and now Entrepreneurs at Play.

Questions families ask

Frequently asked questions.

Don't see yours? Ask it on your free 15-minute call.

Is coaching the same as therapy or psychiatric care?

No. Coaching is practical, non-clinical support for parenting and family life. Psychiatric care is medical care from a physician. On your free call we'll help you figure out which one fits.

Do you prescribe medication?

Coaching and courses don't include medication, and we encourage every family to keep working with their current therapist or medication provider. If your child may need a psychiatric evaluation, we'll talk it through on your free call.

Where do you see families?

In the Omaha area. Coaching sessions can also happen by phone or Zoom. Dr. Che is licensed in Nebraska and Iowa.

Can my school or childcare center offer your services to its families?

Yes. You can offer all of our services, from private coaching to group courses, to the families at your center.

What if I don't belong to a school or center?

You're absolutely welcome. We love working with small groups: playgroups, church groups, book clubs, friends.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the service and the size of your group. We'll share them on your free 15-minute call, with no pressure to decide.

What is the developmental assessment?

For children about 2½ to 9, the Gesell Developmental Observation–Revised (GDO-R) shows where a child is in their growth, so the plan fits them, not an average. How it works.

Do you work with families of every background?

Yes. Our approach honors families of every ethnic and sociocultural background.

Is this for emergencies?

No. If your child is in danger, call 911. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988, any time.
